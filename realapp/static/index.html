BROWSER VOICE SUPPORT

Voice intake & handoff

Customers can start a browser voice support session. LiveKit connects the session and can bring a technician into the conversation.

  • ✓ Browser-based two-way audio
  • ✓ Live conversation transcription when configured
  • ✓ Request a technician during a support call

MULTIMODAL VISION

Visual evidence and analysis

Customers can attach images to incidents. Staff can add supported photos or video during case intake; AI analysis depends on provider and storage configuration.

  • ✓ Customer intake: up to 3 images and 20 MB total
  • ✓ In-line video playback and full-res image modal
  • ✓ AI visual observations for technician review

COMPLIANCE & QA

Automated Quality Audits

From issue report to human-verified resolution

A sample of how intake, AI-assisted investigation, routing, and technician review fit together.

01

Multichannel Intake

Customers describe an issue and attach supported images. Staff can also start with a transcript, audio recording, photos, or video.

02

Gemini Analysis

When configured, Gemini extracts intent, suggests incident fields and urgency, and prepares a draft for staff review.

03

Knowledge & Routing

Vector similarity retrieves matched runbooks, while technician availability and service skills guide incident routing.

04

Resolution & QA

Technicians confirm the cause and resolution. Quality checks can review the incident evidence.
